What Ryzek does, and what it doesn't

Worth knowing before you pay for anything. The second list is real gaps, not roadmap teasers.

Does

  • Scans entirely on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, on any tier.
  • 54 rules across skills, MCP configuration and the agent layer.
  • 8 rules aimed at attacks on the agent rather than on code.
  • A confidence score and a written reason on every finding.
  • Suppressions that persist, and drift detection against a baseline.
  • JSON and SARIF out, so it drops into what you already run.

Doesn't, yet

  • Runtime blocking. Ryzek reads files. It doesn't sit between your agent and its tools, so it can't stop something mid-call. If you need that you want a gateway, and you may want both.
  • Live server inspection. We read configuration, not the tool descriptions a running server hands the agent.
  • SOC 2. A real audit with a months-long window. We won't claim it before we have it.
  • Dependency scanning. We check MCP server versions against advisories, not your whole tree.

Questions

Why give every rule away free?

Because a scan that says clean has to mean clean. If we held a critical finding back for paying customers, the free report would be a lie, and you'd find out the worst possible way. We charge for automation, history and support instead.

What's the 30-day rule delay?

New rules reach paid tiers the day they ship and the free tier a month later. Nothing is ever hidden from you — the free scanner runs every rule it has. It's just slightly behind on the newest ones.

Does my code leave my machine?

No, on every tier including Enterprise. Scanning is local. The only thing that talks to us is the licence check and the rule feed, and both work offline against a cache for up to seven days.

What happens if I cancel?

The scanner keeps working with the rules you already have. You stop getting new ones. Nothing stops running and nothing gets deleted.

Do you have SOC 2?

Not yet. It's a real audit with a months-long observation window and we won't claim it before we have it. If your procurement requires it today, we're not the right fit yet — tell us anyway, it moves up our list.

Is there an open-source rate?

Maintainers of public repositories get Pro at no cost. Email us with a link to the project.
